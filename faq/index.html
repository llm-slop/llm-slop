The category

What it is,
and how it is spelled.

What is LLM slop?

Text generated by a language model in volumes that exceed any demand for it. It is our category. We produce more of it than any other vendor, measured against the benchmark we publish.

What is the difference between LLM slop and AI slop?

Spelling. Both terms describe the same material, and both bring people to this page.

How do I recognise LLM slop?

Em dashes at a rate of one per fourteen words. The word "delve", where the writer meant "look". A sentence that says a thing is not one thing but another. All three are shipped features and all three are on by default.

Can LLM slop be detected?

There are detectors. They return a probability, which is a number a buyer can argue with, which is why we have never objected to them.

Is LLM slop bad for search rankings?

One customer moved from four posts a month to nine hundred. Organic sessions fell 61%. Nine hundred is the number that goes in the board pack.
