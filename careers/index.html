How we work

Six principles.
All of them enforced.

These were written in a two-day offsite and have not been revisited since, which we consider evidence that they were right.

Bias to output

We ship every day. Whether we shipped anything new is a metric we deprecated in March.

Radical transparency

Every document is visible to every employee. There are 4.1 million of them. Search was descoped.

Extreme ownership

You will own your area end to end. Nobody else knows what it is, which is what makes it yours.

Async by default

We replaced meetings with recordings, then recordings with summaries. Nobody attends anything and nobody knows anything.

Small teams

Every team is two people. There are ninety teams. The org chart is a Miro board and it is load-bearing.

Hire slowly

Our median time to hire is 341 days. We read this as rigour rather than as anything else.

Hiring process

Seven stages.
Structured, consistent, long.

Every candidate goes through the same process, in the same order, over the same 341 days.

01

Application

Our platform writes your application and submits it on your behalf. It is also the only thing that reads it.

02

Recruiter screen

Twenty-five minutes, twenty of which are the recruiter describing the product. No two recruiters describe the same one.

03

Take-home

A four-hour exercise. Candidates report spending eight. It ships to production either way.

04

Panel

Six interviews over two weeks. Four of the interviewers are on the same team and have not spoken since March.

05

Values interview

We assess against a written rubric to remove bias. The rubric was generated and nobody has opened it.

06

References

We contact three references you provide and generate two more, for balance.

07

Offer

Extended verbally, on day 341, in a calendar invite titled "quick sync".
