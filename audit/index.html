Fine print

How the audit works.

Runs in your browser

Your paragraph is never uploaded, stored or read. The Compliance Division operates entirely on your device, which is also where it files its expenses.

Scientifically unrigorous

The score combines buzzword density, delve index and sentence inflation using a formula devised during a lunch break. It says nothing about who wrote the text or whether it is any good. The lunch was excellent.

Certificates mean nothing

The downloadable certificate has no legal standing, no educational value and no expiry date. It is, in that sense, perfect.
